Recourse: accident claim demo (AAA, real call to a consenting participant)

Before the demo
1. Start the live server from the repo root with: npm run dev:live
   It reads ~/.config/speedhacks/dial.env (REFUND_MODE=live plus the OpenAI and Dial settings).
   Open http://localhost:3001/ (or whichever port you passed). Do not add ?api=fixture.
   GET /api/runtime must answer mode "live" with extraction and calls ready.
2. Have two files ready: the accident photo and the AAA policy declarations page.
   docs/demo/samples/aaa-auto-policy-declarations.txt is a labeled sample whose claims line is the demo number +1 (949) 520-0007.
   A presenter's own AAA plan works the same way; the claims line it names is the number offered for the call.
3. The phone at +1 949 520 0007 belongs to the person playing the AAA claims representative. Put it on speaker for the room.

During the demo
1. Click New case. Attach both files with Evidence. Type the request and press Enter:
   I was rear-ended yesterday, October 10, 2026, in San Francisco by a silver sedan, California plate 8UFJ426.
   My red 2021 Toyota Camry has rear bumper and trunk damage; no injuries.
   I am the policyholder, insured with AAA (policy on the attached declarations page), and the damage photo is attached.
   Build a plan to file the collision claim with AAA and call the AAA claims line to open the claim and get a claim number.
2. Within about 10 seconds the case facts show AAA Insurance (CSAA Insurance Group) and the claims line read from the plan.
   Within about a minute the case brief fills in: recorded facts with their sources, the evidence index, unknowns, and the action list.
   Measured on 2026-10-11: case created in 7.3 s, analysis complete about 50 s after sending, no clarification round needed.
3. On the action "Review insurer information call" click Review call.
   Pick the AAA contact, set Maximum duration (180 seconds is plenty), tick the consent box, then Authorize call.
   Review the frozen brief, then Place live call. Only that last button dials.
4. The representative answers and plays AAA claims: verify the policy, take the loss report (date, rear-end collision, plate 8UFJ426, photos on file, no injuries), issue a claim number, assign an adjuster, explain next steps (photos to the claim, repair shop, rental, deductible).
5. After the call, Refresh reconciles the result and transcript (a late transcript can take another poll).
   Then send the summary by text from the outcome card.

If the live server cannot be used
Since commit 53c66b6 the page always talks to the real server, so there is no in-browser fixture fallback at runtime.
The scripted AAA accident claim (five-step call plan, AAA rep transcript, claim number 1002-48-7731, texted summary) lives in the fixture engine and its tests under src/features/refund-ui/api/fixture-engine.

Good to know
- The live workflow's insurer call is an information call by design: the agent asks what documents and process apply and does not itself open the claim. The roleplay representative can still issue a claim number on the call.
- Equivalent facts worded two ways (the same phone number in two formats, AAA and its legal name, the policyholder called "insured") no longer count as conflicts since 2026-10-11.
- Cases are owner-cookie scoped in local SQLite; a new browser profile starts empty.

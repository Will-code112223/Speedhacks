# Rights note: duplicate charges

Background for the customer's position, in general terms; not legal advice.
Verify the specifics for the customer's card type, issuer and dates before relying on them.

## Say out loud

"If the duplicate can't be refunded, federal card dispute rules let the customer dispute it with their card issuer, but they would rather you correct it directly."
Say it once, calmly, only after a refusal, and never as a threat.

## Credit cards: Fair Credit Billing Act

- The Fair Credit Billing Act (15 U.S.C. 1666) lets a cardholder dispute a billing error with the card issuer, the bank behind the card, including a charge in the wrong amount.
- The FTC's billing-error guidance includes being billed twice for the same thing.
- The written notice must reach the issuer within 60 days after the first statement showing the error was sent.
- It goes to the billing-inquiries address, not the payment address.
- The issuer must acknowledge it within 30 days and resolve it within two billing cycles, never more than 90 days.
- While the issuer investigates, the cardholder may withhold the disputed amount and related charges but should pay the rest.

## Debit cards: Regulation E

- Regulation E (12 CFR 1005.11) treats an incorrect electronic fund transfer as an error the bank must investigate.
- The bank must hear from the customer within 60 days after it sent the statement showing the error.
- A phone call counts as notice, though the bank may ask for written confirmation within 10 business days.
- The bank generally has 10 business days to investigate.
- If it provisionally credits the account, it may take up to 45 days, or 90 days for debit card purchases at merchants, including by mail or phone.

Neither route needs the merchant's agreement, because the issuer decides.

## Written request

For the customer to send to their card issuer, never read on a call.
The customer adds their own account details.

> I am disputing a billing error on my account.
> My statement dated {statement date} shows two charges of {amount} from {merchant} for one purchase: {original charge} and {duplicate charge}.
> The second charge is a duplicate.
> I asked {merchant} to refund it on {date}, and the response was: {response}.
> Please remove the duplicate and credit any related fees or finance charges.
> Credit card: I am giving notice of a billing error under the Fair Credit Billing Act (15 U.S.C. 1666).
> Debit card: I am giving notice of an error under Regulation E (12 CFR 1005.11).
> Copies of both receipts are attached.

Keep only the line for the card type, and keep a copy of what was sent.

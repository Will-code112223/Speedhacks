# Northwind Mutual claims line

## Before the call

For the case engine only; this section is not pasted into the call.

- Northwind Mutual is a fictional insurer invented for the Recourse demo, not a real company.
- Number: +1 415 555 0162, a fictional 555 number that reaches no one; a live demo dials the consenting test line instead.
- Hours: the automated claims assistant answers 24 hours a day; representatives work weekdays, 8 am to 6 pm Pacific, so call then when escalation may matter.
- Expected counterpart: the automated claims assistant, and a representative only after escalation.

## Menu path

Goals in words: "claims", then "new claim" to file, or "existing claim" for a status or an adjuster visit.
An automated claims assistant answers next and also accepts these goals spoken.

## Can and cannot do

It can open a claim, give a claim's status, and schedule an adjuster visit.
It cannot approve, promise or estimate a payment, change the policy, or discuss settlement amounts.

## Identifiers, in the order asked

1. Policy number: two letters, then eight digits in two groups of four.
2. Date of loss: month, day and year.
3. Claimant name, spelled if asked.

A new claim also needs the type of loss and a one-sentence description.

## Escalation

Say "speak to a representative", "claims representative" or "operator".
After two misrecognitions it offers a transfer itself; say yes.
If it says representatives are closed, stop asking and close with the claim number or what blocks progress.

## Typical next steps after filing

- A claim number on the call: "CL" plus eight digits.
- An acknowledgment with claim forms by email.
- An adjuster call or visit; if the assistant offers a visit time, use the decisions rules.
- A decision once proof of claim is complete, with payment as a separate, later step.

You file and check status, the customer confirms any visit directly, and you never negotiate a settlement amount.
